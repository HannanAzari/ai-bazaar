# 🔭 Icon Lab — The Search for the Face of Nestudio

> The permanent framework for discovering **the visual identity of Nestudio** — the single image people
> emotionally remember. Subordinate to the [World Bible](NESTUDIO_WORLD_BIBLE.md); it uses the
> [ICON_TESTS.md](ICON_TESTS.md) battery and the [Soul](SOUL_OF_NESTUDIO.md), and it is the **parent** of
> [HOUSE_LAB.md](HOUSE_LAB.md) — if the face turns out to be a house, HOUSE_LAB runs that deep sub-tournament;
> if it turns out to be a window, a glow, or a curl of smoke, that is discovered *here.*
>
> **Discovery, not selection.** This document does not choose the icon. It defines the repeatable process by
> which any world — not only Nestudio — discovers its timeless face.

---

## Part 1 — The mission

> **We are not searching for a beautiful house. We are searching for the image someone remembers five years
> later.**

Beauty is common and forgettable. **Identity is rare and permanent.** The face of a world is the thing that
survives when everything else is stripped away — no logo, no UI, no text, no explanation — and still, three
seconds later, a stranger feels *something* and, five years later, can still see it behind their eyes.

The face **may not be a house at all.** It may be:
- the **house** (whole-form), or
- a **component** — the roof-hat, the round door, the chimney's smoke, the mailbox, the path, and above all
  the **Warm Window**, or
- an **atmospheric image** — the **Glow** in a window, fireflies at dusk, a single warm light on a dark
  field, smoke curling at sunset.

The Lab searches **all three scales at once**, because the most memorable brand images are often the smallest
(Apple's bite, Nike's tick, McDonald's arch) — and the strongest candidate the [Soul](SOUL_OF_NESTUDIO.md)
already names is not a building but a feeling made visible: the **Warm Window.** The Lab exists to *prove or
overturn* that hypothesis honestly.

## Part 2 — ~30 icon directions (hypotheses at every scale)

Each is a hypothesis about *what people will remember.* Whole-house directions overlap with the deeper
[HOUSE_LAB](HOUSE_LAB.md) roster and are cross-referenced there, not re-detailed; the **component** and
**atmospheric** directions are unique to this Lab and honor the mission's "it may be the window / the roof /
the chimney / something unimagined."

*Format: Name — Emotional goal · Inspiration · Why someone might love it · Why it might fail · Biggest risk.*
*Scale: 🏠 whole · 🧩 component · 🌫️ atmospheric.*

### 🛋️ Comfort *(owns: safety, warmth, homecoming)*
1. **The Warm Window** 🧩 — *belonging, welcome.* Lit windows seen from a dark street. Universal, wordless, reduces to a single glow. **Fail:** feels like a detail, not a "thing." **Risk:** too simple to be seen as an icon (or: so strong it's the whole answer).
2. **Round Cottage** 🏠 — *cozy archetype.* The house a child draws. Instantly a home. **Fail:** too generic to own. **Risk:** indistinguishable from other cozy games. *(See [HOUSE_LAB](HOUSE_LAB.md) #12.)*
3. **The Glow-in-the-Window** 🌫️ — *someone is home, and it's you.* The living [Glow](THE_CREATOR_SPIRIT.md). Emotional, ownable, alive. **Fail:** hard to read in a still. **Risk:** invisible without motion.
4. **Homecoming Doorway** 🧩 — *the exhale of return.* A warm door ajar, light spilling out. **Fail:** reads as an entrance UI. **Risk:** too transactional.

### ✨ Wonder *(owns: magic, curiosity, "somewhere else")*
5. **Lantern House** 🏠 — *invitation.* A house that *is* a glowing lantern. Unbeatable at night. **Fail:** reads as a lamp. **Risk:** loses the "home." *(HOUSE_LAB #6.)*
6. **Fireflies at a Window** 🌫️ — *small enchantment.* Warm motes by a lit pane. Magical, rare, memorable. **Fail:** too subtle at a glance. **Risk:** gimmick if overused.
7. **Chimney-Smoke Curl** 🧩 — *the world is alive, the hearth is lit.* A slow ribbon of smoke. Signifies life with one stroke. **Fail:** needs the house to mean anything. **Risk:** can't stand alone.
8. **Tiny Observatory** 🏠 — *dreaming upward.* Dome + warm window to the stars. **Fail:** niche. **Risk:** reads as equipment. *(HOUSE_LAB #29.)*

### 🌿 Nature *(owns: rootedness, seasons, being part of the world)*
9. **Pebble House** 🏠 — *serene belonging.* Grown river-stone. DNA-native. **Fail:** reads as only a rock. *(HOUSE_LAB #1.)*
10. **Hill House** 🏠 — *hidden safety.* Burrowed into a green hill. **Fail:** window hides. *(HOUSE_LAB #2.)*
11. **Forest Nest** 🏠 — *retreat.* A warm home among trees. **Fail:** generic if under-styled. *(HOUSE_LAB #4.)*
12. **The Mossy Roof** 🧩 — *loved by time.* A living green roof-cap. *Loved-not-new* made literal. **Fail:** a texture, not an icon. **Risk:** too small a signal.
13. **Tree House** 🏠 — *childhood adventure.* **Fail:** verticality/complexity. *(HOUSE_LAB #28.)*

### 🏛️ Architecture *(owns: craft, intentionality, timeless design)*
14. **The Roof-Hat** 🧩 — *shelter, the big soft hug.* The oversized soft-arch roof silhouette *alone.* Reduces to a memorable mark. **Fail:** ambiguous without a body. **Risk:** reads as a tent/bridge.
15. **Courtyard House** 🏠 — *private sanctuary.* Inward garden heart. **Fail:** hides the Warm Window. *(HOUSE_LAB #11.)*
16. **The Round Door** 🧩 — *welcome, threshold.* A single round arched door. Iconic (cf. hobbit door). **Fail:** strong existing association. **Risk:** derivative.
17. **Artist Loft** 🏠 — *creativity, a maker's light.* **Fail:** big window risks going glassy. *(HOUSE_LAB #15.)*

### 🧚 Fantasy *(owns: escape, story, impossibility)*
18. **Mushroom House** 🏠 — *delight.* Toadstool-cap silhouette. **Fail:** kitsch. *(HOUSE_LAB #3.)*
19. **Floating Nest** 🏠 — *lightness, dream.* Gently hovering. **Fail:** breaks the grounded law. *(HOUSE_LAB #18.)*
20. **Lighthouse** 🏠 — *a beacon that finds you.* A turning warm light. **Fail:** verticality; not a "home." **Risk:** reads industrial/coastal.
21. **Windmill** 🏠 — *gentle living motion.* A moving signature. **Fail:** motion distracts. *(HOUSE_LAB #26.)*

### 🏺 Craft *(owns: handmade, loved-not-new, the human hand)*
22. **Clay House** 🏠 — *handmade tenderness.* Pinch-pot home. DNA-perfect. **Fail:** close to Pebble. *(HOUSE_LAB #7.)*
23. **The Handmade Wobble** 🧩 — *a person made this.* The living-asymmetry imperfection *itself* as identity. **Fail:** too abstract to be a "picture." **Risk:** invisible as an icon.
24. **The Mailbox** 🧩 — *addressed to someone; a little hello.* A characterful path-side sentinel. Lovable, plush-ready. **Fail:** peripheral. **Risk:** too minor to carry the brand.
25. **The Path of Stones** 🧩 — *the walk toward welcome.* Rounded stepping stones to a door. **Fail:** means nothing without the destination. **Risk:** not a standalone image.
26. **Greenhouse** 🏠 — *growing, tending, light.* A warm glass-and-frame grow-home. **Fail:** glassiness fights the DNA. **Risk:** cold/product-like.

### ⚪ Minimal *(owns: calm, essence, restraint)*
27. **Monolith Nest** 🏠 — *quiet mystery.* One soft mass + one window. **Fail:** reads as an object. *(HOUSE_LAB #22.)*
28. **Tiny House** 🏠 — *contentment, "enough."* **Fail:** feels slight. *(HOUSE_LAB #20.)*
29. **Dome Nest** 🏠 — *primal calm.* Half-sphere shelter; snow beacon. **Fail:** featureless without a great window. *(HOUSE_LAB #21.)*
30. **A Single Glow on a Dark Field** 🌫️ — *hope, homecoming, "there.".* The most reduced Nestudio image: one warm point of light in soft dark. The ultimate logo/emoji candidate. **Fail:** too abstract to read as *home.* **Risk:** could be any light — needs the tiniest hint of a window/roof to become Nestudio.

> **The whole-house directions are the [HOUSE_LAB](HOUSE_LAB.md) roster; the component and atmospheric
> directions are unique to the Icon Lab** — because the face of a world is often smaller than a building.

## Part 3 — The seven emotional families (what territory each owns)

- **Comfort** — safety, warmth, homecoming. *The core of Nestudio; whatever wins must live here.*
- **Wonder** — magic, curiosity, the sense of *somewhere else worth going.*
- **Nature** — rootedness and seasons; the feeling of *belonging to a living world.*
- **Architecture** — craft and intentionality; *this was designed by a careful hand.*
- **Fantasy** — escape and story; *impossible in the best way.*
- **Craft** — the human hand, *loved-not-new*, the anti-mass-production signal.
- **Minimal** — calm and essence; *the icon that survives being reduced to almost nothing.*

The families exist so the search covers the **whole emotional map**, not one designer's reflex. The winner
must sit unmistakably in **Comfort** and borrow strength from one or two neighbours (Wonder, Craft, Nature).
An icon that lives only in Fantasy, Architecture, or Minimal will be admired but not *loved.*

## Part 4 — The elimination process

```
30  →  15  →  8  →  4  →  2  →  1
```
**Never eliminate for beauty. Eliminate only for emotional weakness.** Each round removes candidates that
fail *one emotional question.*

| Round | Cut | Question | Cut what fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 · The Glance** | 30 → 15 | Does it stop a stranger in three seconds and create longing? | the invisible / the ignorable |
| **R2 · The Five-Year Memory** | 15 → 8 | Would they still see it behind their eyes in five years? | the forgettable |
| **R3 · The Reduction** | 8 → 4 | Does it survive becoming a logo and an emoji? | what needs detail to exist |
| **R4 · The Seasons & Solitude** | 4 → 2 | Beautiful *and* safe in rain, snow, sunset, fog, night, and alone on a lonely road? | the fair-weather icon |
| **R5 · The Uncopyable** | 2 → 1 | If a rival copied the exact gameplay, which one could they never copy? | the copyable |

The final round is decided by **Part 8** — the icon is whatever remains *uncopyable.* Fusion is allowed (a
component icon may merge with a whole-form one — e.g. "the Warm Window *of* the Round Cottage"). Every cut is
recorded with its reason and archived, never deleted.

## Part 5 — The test battery (invent stronger tests)

Reuses the permanent [ICON_TESTS.md](ICON_TESTS.md) battery (Empty World, Child Drawing, Winter Night, Rain,
Returning Home, Screenshot, "I Wish This Existed", One Hour, Friend, Vacation — cross-referenced, not
restated) and adds the following **icon-specific tests.** Each defines PASS / FAIL.

| Test | PASS | FAIL |
|---|---|---|
| **The Five-Year Memory Test** | a person who saw it once can still describe/draw it five years later | it blurs into "some cozy game" within a week |
| **The Stop-Scrolling Test** | a thumb pauses on it mid-scroll, involuntarily | it slides past like everything else |
| **The Warm Window Test** | the eye goes *straight* to a warm glow and feels welcomed | there is no single warm point to land on; the eye wanders |
| **The Snow Test** | under snow it becomes *more* magical and the window a brighter beacon | snow makes it cold, bleak, or generic |
| **The Sunset Test** | at golden hour it is the most beautiful it ever looks | sunset adds nothing; it looks the same or worse |
| **The Lonely Road Test** | seen small and alone in an empty landscape, it makes you *ache to go toward it* | alone, it looks lost, sad, or unremarkable |
| **The Logo Test** | one flat monochrome mark still says "Nestudio home" | it becomes an unreadable blob or a generic shape |
| **The LEGO Test** | it reduces to a few chunky blocks and still reads as itself | it needs fine detail to be recognizable |
| **The Plush Toy Test** | you want to *hug* it; it's huggable as an object | it's flat, cold, or has nothing to hold |
| **The Emoji Test** | at 16×16 px it is still unmistakable | it turns to mush; could be any little house |
| **The Souvenir Test** | you'd want it as a fridge magnet / keychain / sticker | you'd never keep a memento of it |
| **The Homesick Test** *(new)* | seeing it gives a pang of *missing a place you've never been* | it evokes no longing, only appraisal |
| **The Wordless Test** *(new)* | it needs zero text to communicate everything it means | it only works with a caption/label |
| **The Doodle Test** *(new)* | someone would idly draw it in a notebook margin | no one would ever doodle it |
| **The Tattoo Test** *(new, the ultimate)* | someone would tattoo it because of what it *means to them* | unthinkable — it's just a picture |

**The recognition principle:** the smaller the abstraction at which an image still says *"Nestudio home,"* the
more truly it is the icon. The face of a world passes the Emoji, Logo, and Tattoo tests — most beautiful
houses do not.

## Part 6 — The review boards *(HTML, empty instruments — nothing generated this sprint)*

Five board *types*, all built as empty scaffolds now and filled starting M30. Shared board:
`public/test-photos/icon-lab-board.html`.

- **Round board** — the current field as cards: *Icon · Silhouette · Emotion · Pros · Cons · Decision* (the
  Part-2 grid; later rounds reuse it with fewer cards).
- **Comparison board** — two-to-four finalists side by side at equal size, for direct emotional A/B.
- **Silhouette board** — every candidate filled solid black (the gate; reuses the flood-key technique).
- **Weather board** — one candidate across rain · snow · sunset · fog · night, to run the Seasons & Solitude
  round.
- **Memory board** — each candidate shown once, then hidden; the reviewer *draws it from memory*, and the
  drawing is compared to the original (the Child-Drawing / Five-Year-Memory instrument).

## Part 8 — How We Know We Have Found It

Imagine a person accidentally sees **one screenshot** of Nestudio. Three seconds. No logo, no UI, no text, no
explanation. We have found the icon when **all of this happens without a single word:**

- **What must happen emotionally** — an involuntary softening; a small ache of longing and welcome at once —
  the pull of a warm window seen from a cold street. Not admiration ("nice art"). **Desire** ("I want to be
  there").
- **What thoughts should appear** — *"where is that?" · "I wish I could go there" · "that looks like
  somewhere safe."* Thoughts about a **place**, never about a **product.**
- **What they remember tomorrow** — one image and one feeling: *a small warm light, and the wish to be
  inside it.* Not a feature, not a UI, not a colour scheme — a **place-feeling.**
- **What they remember five years later** — the same single image, softened into memory the way a childhood
  window or a grandmother's porch is remembered: *warmth, safety, a light left on for them.* If they can
  still see it, the icon is real.

**The decisive question — the Uncopyable Test:** if another company copied the *exact* gameplay, systems, and
features, **what part could they never copy?** They can copy mechanics, UI, even art style. What they cannot
copy is the **specific emotional image Nestudio owns** — the feeling that *someone left a warm light on for
you, and the place will be glad when you come home.* **That feeling, made into a single picture, is the real
Nestudio icon.** Everything in the Lab is a hypothesis about what that picture is; the leading hypothesis (from
the [Soul](SOUL_OF_NESTUDIO.md)) is the **Warm Window / the Glow** — and the Lab's whole job is to test it
against 29 rivals, honestly, until one image is left that no one could ever copy.

> **We have found it when a stranger, given three silent seconds, doesn't think "what a nice app" — they
> think "I want to go home there," about a home they have never seen.**

## Part 9 — Relationship to the House Lab

- **Icon Lab (this)** searches for the **face** — at every scale, house or not.
- **[House Lab](HOUSE_LAB.md)** runs the deep tournament *within the house form*, invoked if (and only if)
  the face turns out to be a whole house.
- They share the [ICON_TESTS](ICON_TESTS.md) battery, the archive-never-delete rule, and the "judge emotion,
  never beauty" law. Run the Icon Lab first; it decides *what kind of thing* the icon is, then hands off.

---

## Success

The deliverable is **not a house and not an icon** — it is a **repeatable process for discovering a timeless
visual identity**, general enough that a future team could point it at an entirely different world and
discover *that* world's face the same way. A designer five years from now can run this Lab and arrive at the
uncopyable image — without ever being told which one Nestudio chose.
