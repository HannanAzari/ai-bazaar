# Visual DNA

> **Authority:** subordinate to [NESTUDIO_WORLD_BIBLE.md](NESTUDIO_WORLD_BIBLE.md).
> **Companion to:** [RENDERING_DNA.md](RENDERING_DNA.md) (the technical execution — palette hex, lighting,
> camera), [PHYSICS_DNA.md](PHYSICS_DNA.md) (behaviour), [GEOMETRIC_ALPHABET.md](GEOMETRIC_ALPHABET.md) (form).
> **Inherits palette/lighting/philosophy from** [`../nestudio-visual-dna.md`](../nestudio-visual-dna.md)
> (the V1.0 world DNA lock — still authoritative for the *world*; this doc is its governed umbrella).

The permanent visual **qualities** every Nestudio object, scene and interface must carry. Each is stated as
**WHAT** (the rule) and **WHY** (the reason it exists) — a future designer who understands the *why* can
make correct new decisions the rules don't literally cover.

The one-sentence test for anything visual: *"Is this a warm, rounded, matte, hand-made little world?"*

---

## 1. Softness

**WHAT.** Every edge is softened; every corner carries a subtle radius; nothing is sharp, angular, faceted
or CAD-precise. One signature radius is shared across a form so joints read as one hand.

**WHY.** Sharpness reads as *manufactured, clinical, AI-3D*. Softness is the fastest, most universal signal
of "designed by a warm human hand" and is what makes objects feel touchable and safe.

## 2. Warmth

**WHAT.** A restrained warm palette (parchment/plaster/stone base), a single warm key light (`#fff6e0`),
and a cool-plum shadow (`#46365a`). The *light* is warm; objects keep their true colours.

**WHY.** Warmth is the emotional core — it turns "an asset" into "a place I want to live in." The warm
light / cool-plum shadow two-tone is the single strongest unifier across the whole world; drop it and the
family instantly fractures.

## 3. Premium feeling

**WHAT.** Matte, calm, resolved, restrained. No gloss, no clutter, no gimmicks, no more than one accent per
object. Considered proportions; nothing accidental.

**WHY.** Premium is *earned by restraint*, not by adding detail. Cheapness reads as busyness, glossiness,
and trend-chasing. Calm restraint is what lets the world feel valuable and age gracefully.

## 4. Playfulness

**WHAT.** Friendly, characterful proportions; gentle personality in stance and silhouette; a light sense of
delight (calm exaggeration, impossible stability). Playful, never zany.

**WHY.** Playfulness is what makes people *smile and want to explore*. But Nestudio's playfulness is
**quiet** — premium and warm — never meme, novelty or cartoon. Too much kills the premium feeling (§3).

## 5. Handcrafted imperfections

**WHAT.** Tiny, intentional, calm irregularities — living asymmetry, a wavering rim, a hair-fuller side.
Present but never sloppy, never comedic.

**WHY.** Perfect symmetry reads as machine/AI; visible imperfection is the fingerprint of *one artist's
hand*, which is exactly the feeling that cannot be cheaply copied. It is the anti-AI signal.

## 6. Friendly proportions

**WHAT.** Chunkier, rounder, more generous than reality; bodies plump and inflated; bases confident.
Proportions pushed toward warmth, held back from caricature.

**WHY.** Friendly proportions make objects *lovable and huggable* rather than correct. Desirability over
accuracy — people keep and show off things they love, not things that are accurate.

## 7. Readable silhouettes

**WHAT.** Every object must be identifiable — and recognizably Nestudio — from its **filled black
silhouette alone** (the 3-second silhouette test). Silhouette before detail, always.

**WHY.** Silhouette is the deepest layer of brand recognition (Apple, LEGO, Nintendo pass it; a texture
does not). A shared silhouette language across mug/lamp/chair/house/tree is the **moat** — the thing that
makes the world instantly recognizable and very hard to copy. See [GEOMETRIC_ALPHABET.md](GEOMETRIC_ALPHABET.md).

## 8. Calmness

**WHAT.** Even, soft light; gentle contrast; restful compositions; no drama, no urgency, no visual noise.

**WHY.** Nestudio is a place you *live in and return to*, not a poster that shouts once. Calm is what makes
a world habitable and re-visitable, and what lets it age without feeling dated.

## 9. Negative space

**WHAT.** Generous, even margins; openings and cut-outs drawn as deliberate, calm, rounded shapes; nothing
crowded. Negative space is a first-class design element (see [PHYSICS_DNA.md](PHYSICS_DNA.md) §6).

**WHY.** Space is where the object *breathes* and where premium calm lives. Crowding is the fastest way to
make a world feel cheap, busy and stressful. Removing is a design act; decorating is not.

---

## Brand personality (priority order — earlier wins in a conflict)

**1 Cozy · 2 Crafted · 3 Characterful · 4 Calm · 5 Welcoming.**

When two qualities pull against each other, the earlier one wins. (E.g. if *characterful* (playful) starts
to fight *cozy/crafted*, dial the character back.)

## Typography & accessibility (inherited)

- **Type:** Fraunces (diegetic / voice), Nunito Sans (chrome / UI).
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA — body ≥ 4.5:1, large ≥ 3:1; legible at 64 px and 128 px; tap targets ≥ 44×44 px;
  reduced-motion safe.
