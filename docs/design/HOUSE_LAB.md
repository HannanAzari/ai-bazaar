# 🔬 House Lab — The Search for the Icon

> The permanent **experimentation framework** for discovering the first iconic Nestudio house. Subordinate to
> the [World Bible](NESTUDIO_WORLD_BIBLE.md); it operationalizes the *hypotheses* in [HOUSE_DNA.md](HOUSE_DNA.md),
> [SOUL_OF_NESTUDIO.md](SOUL_OF_NESTUDIO.md) and [ICON_TESTS.md](ICON_TESTS.md) into a repeatable search.
>
> **Parent framework:** [ICON_LAB.md](ICON_LAB.md) searches for Nestudio's *face* at every scale and invokes
> this house sub-tournament only if the face turns out to be a whole house. Read Icon Lab first.
>
> **This document does NOT choose a winner. It defines how a winner is discovered.** The actual exploration
> begins in M30. Five years from now a new designer should be able to read this and know *exactly how
> Nestudio discovers iconic architecture* — without knowing which house won.

---

## Part 1 — Purpose

> **We are no longer designing houses. We are searching for the first iconic Nestudio house.**

M23–M28 gave us hypotheses — Rendering DNA, Shape DNA, Geometry DNA, the Living World, the House DNA
philosophy, the Soul. **Hypotheses are not truth until they survive contact with a stranger's eye.** The
question is no longer *"what should a Nestudio house be?"* It is:

> **"What house makes strangers want to enter?"**

This is the first sprint where **documentation follows experimentation** instead of leading it. The Lab is
the instrument; the houses are the specimens; the stranger's involuntary *"I want to be there"* is the only
verdict that counts.

## Part 3 — Why the seven categories exist

Categories are not decoration; each isolates a **different hypothesis about the source of "home-longing":**

- **Natural** — tests whether *belonging comes from feeling grown-from-the-earth* (refuge, rootedness).
- **Playful** — tests whether *character and toy-like charm* create the strongest pull (delight, huggability).
- **Architectural** — tests whether *considered design and archetype* win (universality, craft).
- **Fantasy** — tests whether *magic and impossibility* create longing (wonder, escape).
- **Minimal** — tests whether *restraint and essence* out-perform detail (calm, premium).
- **Historic** — tests whether *timeless vernacular* ages best (endurance, nostalgia).
- **Experimental** — deliberately probes *the edges and the DNA's breaking points* (learning the boundaries).

Spreading the 30 across all seven guarantees the search covers the whole emotional space, not one designer's
reflex. The winner may come from anywhere — or be a *fusion* the tournament reveals.

## Part 4 — The elimination tournament

```
30  →  15  →  8  →  4  →  2  →  1
```

**Never judge beauty. Judge emotion.** Each round asks one emotional question; a house is cut when it fails
*that* question, however pretty it is.

| Round | Cut | The one question judged | Test drawn from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 · The Glance** | 30 → 15 | *Does it stop a stranger scrolling and create longing in three seconds?* | [Screenshot Test](ICON_TESTS.md) |
| **R2 · The Memory** | 15 → 8 | *Would a child draw it, and would an adult still remember it tomorrow?* | [Child Drawing Test](ICON_TESTS.md) |
| **R3 · The Welcome** | 8 → 4 | *Does its Warm Window make you want to walk in?* | [House Exploration](HOUSE_EXPLORATION.md) + Soul |
| **R4 · The Seasons** | 4 → 2 | *Is it beautiful AND safe in rain, snow, fog, sunset and night?* | [Winter Night / Rain Tests](ICON_TESTS.md) |
| **R5 · The Icon** | 2 → 1 | *Can it become the permanent brand icon — LEGO, logo, plush, emoji — and pass every soul test?* | Reducibility + full [ICON_TESTS](ICON_TESTS.md) |

Rules of the tournament:
- **Emotion beats craft, always.** A technically flawless house that stirs nothing loses to a rougher house
  that makes a stranger ache to enter.
- **Silhouette is a gate, not a tiebreaker** — any house that fails the black-silhouette read is cut early.
- **Fusion is allowed.** If two survivors each hold half the answer, the tournament may *merge* them into a
  new specimen for the next round (recorded as a new direction).
- **Every cut is documented** (board "Decision" + reason) — cuts are learning material, never deleted
  (mirrors the [Furniture Library](FURNITURE_LIBRARY.md) archive rule).

## Part 5 — Evaluation criteria

Two families of criteria. Neither is a beauty score.

**A. The emotional battery** — the permanent [ICON_TESTS.md](ICON_TESTS.md), applied to each house:
Screenshot · "I Wish This Existed" · Child Drawing · Winter Night · Rain · Returning Home · One Hour ·
Friend · Vacation · Empty World. (Cross-referenced, not restated.)

**B. The reducibility tests** — can the house survive being *crushed down to an icon?* An iconic house is one
that stays recognizable at every level of abstraction:

| Test | Pass = |
|---|---|
| **Can it become a LEGO?** | it reduces to a few chunky blocks and still reads as itself |
| **Can it become a logo?** | a single flat monochrome mark still says "Nestudio home" |
| **Can it become a plush toy?** | it's huggable — you'd want to hold it |
| **Can it become an emoji?** | at 16×16 px it's still unmistakable |

Plus the round questions of Part 4 (stop scrolling · want to enter · remember tomorrow · revisit · works in
weather). **A house that cannot become a logo cannot become the icon** — reducibility is the hard gate of the
final round.

## Part 6 — Visual review boards

Each tournament round has an **HTML review board** (built as empty scaffolds this sprint — nothing is
generated yet; they are the *instruments*, filled in M30). Every board compares, per house, six columns:

> **House · Silhouette · Emotion · Pros · Cons · Decision**

- **House** — the generated render (placeholder until M30).
- **Silhouette** — the black-fill silhouette (the gate; reuses the flood-key technique from prior boards).
- **Emotion** — the single dominant feeling it creates (pre-filled from Part 2; verified against the render).
- **Pros / Cons** — filled during review.
- **Decision** — ADVANCE / CUT (+ reason). Cuts are archived, never deleted.

Boards this sprint: `public/test-photos/house-lab-board.html` — the full tournament bracket + the Round-1
template grid of all 30 directions as empty review cards, ready to run. Later rounds reuse the same template
with fewer cards.

## Part 7 — The generation roadmap *(roadmap only — nothing generated this sprint)*

The order the exploration will run, starting M30. **No prompts here — only the sequence.**

| Stage | Action | Gate before continuing |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Generate all **30** house directions (specimens) | all 30 rendered & boarded |
| **2** | **Review** R1 — the Glance | board filled |
| **3** | **Destroy 15** → keep 15; improve survivors | 15 advance, cuts archived |
| **4** | **Improve survivors** & run R2–R5 to a single winner | 1 icon chosen |
| **5** | Generate the winner in **weather** (rain, snow, fog, wind) | passes Rain + Seasons |
| **6** | Generate the winner at **night** (Warm Window, lanterns, moonlight) | passes Winter Night |
| **7** | Generate **paths, garden, mailbox, lanterns** (the approach) | passes House Exploration |
| **8** | Generate **interiors** (crossing the threshold) | passes first-30-seconds |
| **9** | Generate the **village** (many homes as one family) | passes Empty World + family test |

Each stage is a full generate → review → cut cycle in batches (never all at once), exactly like
[Craft Cycle 01](FURNITURE_LIBRARY.md). **The winner is not chosen until Stage 4 — and not in this document.**

## Success

At the end of M29 the laboratory exists: **thirty specimens, seven hypotheses, a five-round emotional
tournament, two families of tests, empty review boards, and a nine-stage roadmap.** A designer joining in five
years can pick this up and run the search — and will discover *the same kind of icon* even if they never learn
which house M30 crowned. **That process is now part of the Nestudio DNA forever.** The exploration begins in
M30; this sprint chose *how to choose*, and nothing more.
