# 🏠 House DNA

> **The most important document in the project.** The Nestudio house is to Nestudio what the bitten apple is
> to Apple, the red curve to Coca-Cola, the castle to Disney: **the single icon the whole brand lives or dies
> by.** Subordinate to the [World Bible](NESTUDIO_WORLD_BIBLE.md); it applies the [Geometric
> Alphabet](GEOMETRIC_ALPHABET.md) (Pebble · Arch · Capsule), the [Rendering DNA](RENDERING_DNA.md), and the
> [Living World](LIVING_WORLD.md) to one sacred object. **Emotional laws, not implementation.**
>
> The house exists to be the **Warm Window** made architecture (see [Soul](SOUL_OF_NESTUDIO.md)) — the thing
> a person sees for three seconds and *wishes were real.*

---

## The Child Drawing Test (the master test for the house)

If a child saw a Nestudio house once and drew it from memory, they would draw:

- a **soft, rounded, wider-than-tall little body** — a house you could hug;
- a **big generous arched roof** that sits on it like a warm hat, almost too big, pulled down snug;
- **one glowing window**, warm gold, unmistakably *lit from within*;
- a **small arched door**;
- a **little chimney** with a single soft **curl of smoke**;
- the whole thing **nestled into the ground**, with a small **path** leading to the door.

**If those six things are present and rounded and warm, it is a Nestudio house. If any is missing, it is
not.** Everything else is variation; these are the DNA.

## The laws that never change

1. **Wider than tall. Low and grounded.** A Nestudio house is a **lap, never a tower.** It sits close to the
   earth, slightly nestled in, as if it grew there. Verticality reads as ambition, status, city — the
   opposite of cozy. The house must always feel like it wants you to sit down.
2. **The roof is a big soft hug.** A generous rounded **Arch** roof, roughly *half the house's height*,
   pulled down over the body like a hat two sizes too big. The roof is the single most recognizable shape —
   oversized, soft-cornered, protective. A thin or sharp or flat roof is not Nestudio.
3. **One Warm Window is the soul.** Every Nestudio house has a **window that glows gold from inside** — the
   emotional center of the entire brand. It is never dark, never cold, never empty-black. The window is how
   the house says *someone is home and you are welcome.* If you remember one thing about a Nestudio house,
   it is this light. **Protect the Warm Window above all else.**
4. **Everything is one soft radius.** Body, roof, door, window, chimney — all share a single gentle corner
   radius, so the whole house reads as *one hand, one breath, one lump of warm clay.* (Physics DNA:
   internal inflation — the house looks gently filled with air and warmth.)
5. **A curl of smoke means life.** A slow, soft ribbon of chimney smoke is the house *breathing* (Living
   World: everything breathes). It says the hearth is lit, the [Glow](THE_CREATOR_SPIRIT.md) is home. Smoke
   is calm and slow — never a plume, never industrial.
6. **It grows from the ground, with a path to the door.** A small welcoming **path** and a soft skirt of
   ground/plants at the base. The house is never a floating object on a void; it *belongs* to its spot.

## The parts — how each should *feel*

- **Windows** — warm, glowing, alive; small and few, never a glass facade. A window is an *eye of welcome*,
  not a view. Its gold light is the most precious pixel in Nestudio. At most one or two; more than that and
  the house stops feeling like a home and starts feeling like a building.
- **Door** — a small soft **Arch**, slightly rounded and inviting, a little worn at the threshold from being
  loved and used. The door should feel like it *wants* to open (Living World: opening is a welcome).
- **Roof** — the big soft hat (Law 2). It may carry gentle texture (thatch, shingle, tile) but never sharp
  ridges, never hard geometry, never a flat modern plane.
- **Chimney** — a small confident **Capsule**, off-centre with living asymmetry, wearing its curl of smoke.
  A house without a chimney can feel unlit; the chimney is the promise of a fire.
- **Garden** — a small, gentle, cared-for skirt of green: a few rounded plants, never a manicured lawn,
  never wild overgrowth. The garden says *someone tends this place.*
- **Path** — short, soft, curving slightly, made of rounded stepping stones or worn earth. It exists to be
  *walked* (see [House Exploration](HOUSE_EXPLORATION.md)); it draws the eye and the heart to the door.
- **Mailbox** — a tiny, lovable, characterful sentinel at the path's start — the house's little handshake,
  its "hello" before you arrive. Rounded, warm, one accent. It makes the Nest feel *addressed to someone.*
- **Lanterns** — small, warm, gold — the Warm Window's companions along the path and door. They are how the
  house welcomes you *in the dark* (Living World: darkness tucks in). Never harsh, never many.
- **Plants** — rounded, soft, a little wild-but-tended, always at the base and edges — the house softening
  into its world. They breathe and sway (World Atmosphere).

## How the house ages, glows, and weathers

- **Aging** — *loved, not new* ([Living World, Materials](LIVING_WORLD.md#chapter-3--materials-defined-emotionally)).
  A Nestudio house looks gently cared-for and a little worn — softened corners, a mossy roof edge, a
  well-trodden threshold. It never looks brand-new (soulless) and never looks derelict (sad). It looks
  *lived in.*
- **Night** — the house comes into its full soul at night: the **Warm Window blazes gentle gold**, lanterns
  wake, the smoke catches the moonlight, the body falls into safe plum-blue shadow. **Night is the house's
  most beautiful hour**, and it must never be scary — a Nestudio house at night is the safest image in the
  world (see the [Winter Night Test](ICON_TESTS.md)).
- **Weather** — weather is comfort and wonder ([Living World, Time](LIVING_WORLD.md#chapter-4--time)). In
  rain, the window glows warmer against the grey and you *wish you were inside.* In snow, the roof wears a
  soft white cap and the window is a beacon of warmth. In wind, the smoke and plants sway. In fog, the house
  becomes an intimate glow drawing you close. Weather never threatens the house; it makes you long for its
  inside.

## What must NEVER be added to a Nestudio house

- **A dark or empty window.** The gravest violation — a cold window kills the soul.
- **Sharp, flat, angular, or modern-glass architecture.** No towers, no cubes, no glass facades, no brutalism.
- **Height/verticality as status.** No mansions, no skyscrapers — the house is a lap, not a monument.
- **More than one accent colour**, garish paint, or a busy, cluttered facade.
- **Realistic/photographic detail, gloss, or a product-render look.** (Rendering DNA: matte, hand-painted.)
- **Signage, logos, UI, badges, or numbers** stuck on the house — it is a home, not a storefront.
- **Anything that reads as a *building* rather than a *home*** — offices, shops, institutions.
- **Menacing weather, hard shadows, or a scary night.** The house is always safe.

## Why it is impossible to confuse with any other world

Other games have cute houses. Nestudio's house is unmistakable because it fuses **five signatures at once**:
the **oversized soft-arch roof-hat**, the **wider-than-tall grounded lap-body**, the **single glowing Warm
Window as the emotional center**, the **slow curl of living smoke**, and the **matte hand-painted one-radius
finish** — all in service of one feeling no competitor is aiming for: *a warm window you ache to walk into.*
A rival can copy any one of these; copying the **feeling** requires copying the entire Bible. That is the
moat.

> **The House DNA in one line:** *a rounded little lap of a house under a big soft roof-hat, with one gold
> window that means someone is home — and it's you.*
