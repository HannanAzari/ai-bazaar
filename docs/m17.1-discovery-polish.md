## Notes on the engagement/social numbers

`lib/nest-engagement.ts` derives **deterministic** placeholder counts from a stable id/handle, so
they never flicker and never imply live data. Every control that shows them is **disabled** (likes/
comments/follow) or does something real & local (Share copies the link). Owner stats + profile
followers/following carry an explicit "placeholders" note / are clearly non-interactive. **No
tables, no backend.**

## What was intentionally NOT changed

Single editor `/nest-editor`, M16 identity/account **ownership**, local draft **migration**, and
the M17 discovery model are preserved. No follows/comments/**real likes** backend, no notifications
backend, no villages/marketplace/AI, no auth rewrite, no DB migrations.

## Known limitations

- Engagement + follower/following counts are **placeholders** (UI only) until a social backend.
- A curated `?c=` share link still decodes a compact doc (no owner/template); the visitor page now
  also checks the **local** published record by slug, so the OWNER is recognised on their own
  public Nest, but a *cross-browser* visitor of a `?c=` curated link sees the default creator.
- Real analytics + real thumbnails-at-scale land with the Supabase cutover.
