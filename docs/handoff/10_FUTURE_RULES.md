# 10 · FUTURE RULES

> The permanent working rules for every future Nestudio session — AI or human. Short. Memorable. Non-negotiable.

## How we work
1. **Documentation must unblock implementation.** If a doc doesn't help something get built, don't write it.
2. **Never create new philosophy documents** unless implementation exposes a real gap. Philosophy lives in `03`.
3. **Every sprint produces something the founder can experience** — an image, a room, a working interaction. Not just words.
4. **Generate before scaling. Quality before quantity.** Prove it small; gate; then scale.
5. **Honesty over polish.** Report what actually happened — failures, costs, gaps — never a rosier story.
6. **Nothing ships without the founder.** No freeze, tag, commit, push, or scale without a clear go.

## What is singular (never fork)
7. **One interaction language. One camera. One design language. One sound language.**

## What we're building
8. **Objects before features.** The vocabulary is the product; features serve it.
9. **The room before the village.** Get one Nest perfect before any social/spatial layer.
10. **Visitors before metrics. Curiosity before engagement.** Design for the person exploring, not the dashboard.
11. **Products become identity. Places accumulate life.** Businesses and memories obey the same soul.
12. **If a new object doesn't add a letter to the alphabet of identity, it doesn't ship.**

## The stance
> Coherence by construction. Restraint as luxury. Warmth over flash.
> Build the alphabet of digital identity — and make every Nest beautiful before anyone edits it.
